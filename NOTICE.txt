Games adapted from Overland In A Box (OIAB)
  Adapted by the repository owner from his OIAB project. The project-specific
  portions included in Daemoncade are released here under GPLv3.

  iMines, Checkers, Tic-Tac-Toe, Blockfall, Connect Four, Dots and Boxes,
  Hangman, and Chess originated in or were adapted from OIAB. Network play,
  appliance profiles, server statistics, and network score submission were
  removed for these standalone browser editions.
  The public edition removes OIAB profiles, server statistics, and appliance APIs.

Games adapted from DaemonOS
  Adapted from DaemonOS, Copyright Jason Daemon.
  DaemonOS source is licensed under the GNU General Public License, version 3.
  Source: https://github.com/jasondaemon/daemonos
  License: https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-3.0.txt

  Snake, Paddle Duel, Space Rocks, Pinball, Casey, Missiles Away,
  Chilopodophobia, Road Hopper, Jungle Jumper, Blitz!, Racecar, Spacefighter,
  and Space Defender were adapted from DaemonOS. Server integration and
  network score submission were removed. Space Defender retains three
  project-owned, Suno-generated sound effects from DaemonOS.

Planck.js
  Pinball bundles Planck.js 0.3.0 by Ali Shakiba and Erin Catto under the zlib

PixiJS
  Blockfall, Canyon Crawler, Orbit Run, and iMines use the local PixiJS 7.4.3 distribution under
  the MIT License. See games/vendor/pixi/LICENSE.txt.
  Source: https://github.com/pixijs/pixijs

Phaser
  Neon Breaker and Circuit Rush use the local Phaser 3.90.0 Arcade
  Physics distribution by Phaser Studio Inc. under the MIT License. See
  games/vendor/phaser/LICENSE.md.
  Source: https://github.com/phaserjs/phaser
  Its Neon Sector, Armored Sector, Hypergrid, and Guardian Core music is
  project-owned and generated with Suno. Web editions are loudness-normalized
  derivatives of the source WAV files.

Three.js
  Blitz!, Racecar, and Space Defender use the local Three.js r185 (0.185.1 distribution)

  games/vendor/three/LICENSE.txt.
  Source: https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js

Kenney City Kit (Industrial)
  Blitz! includes selected industrial buildings, tanks, towers, chimneys, and

  the models in games/blitz/assets/models/industrial/LICENSE.txt.
  Source: https://kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-industrial

Quaternius Toon Shooter Game Kit
  Blitz! includes the Soldier and Enemy animated character models from the

  Source: https://quaternius.com/packs/toonshootergamekit.html

JavaScript Racer
  Racecar's earlier pseudo-3D renderer was adapted from Jake Gordon's
  JavaScript Racer, distributed under the MIT License. The current renderer
  uses fixed world geometry and a Three.js perspective camera; this credit is
  retained for the earlier implementation and its influence.
  Source: https://github.com/jakesgordon/javascript-racer

Kenney Racing Pack
  Circuit Rush includes selected vehicle and trackside art from Kenney's
  Racing Pack, released under Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0).
  Source: https://kenney.nl/assets/racing-pack

The public editions use no external runtime services or remotely loaded
libraries.
