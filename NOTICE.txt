Games adapted from Overland In A Box (OIAB)
  Adapted by the repository owner from his OIAB project. The project-specific
  portions included in Daemoncade are released here under GPLv3.

  Mines, Checkers, Tic-Tac-Toe, Blockfall, Connect Four, Dots and Boxes,
  Hangman, and Chess originated in or were adapted from OIAB. Network play,
  appliance profiles, server statistics, and network score submission were
  removed for these standalone browser editions.
  The public edition removes OIAB profiles, server statistics, and appliance APIs.

Games adapted from DaemonOS
  Adapted from DaemonOS, Copyright Jason Daemon.
  DaemonOS source is licensed under the GNU General Public License, version 3.
  Source: https://github.com/jasondaemon/daemonos
  License: https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-3.0.txt

  Snake, Paddle Duel, Space Rocks, Pinball, Casey, Missiles Away,
  Chilopodophobia, Road Hopper, Jungle Jumper, Blitz!, Racecar, Spacefighter,
  and Space Defender were adapted from DaemonOS. Server integration and
  network score submission were removed. Space Defender retains three
  project-owned, Suno-generated sound effects from DaemonOS.

Phaser
  Neon Breaker, Racecar, and Circuit Rush use the local Phaser 3.90.0 Arcade
  Physics distribution by Phaser Studio Inc. under the MIT License. See
  games/vendor/phaser/LICENSE.md.
  Source: https://github.com/phaserjs/phaser
