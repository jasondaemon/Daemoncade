Games adapted from Overland In A Box (OIAB)
  Adapted by the repository owner from his OIAB project. The project-specific
  portions included in Daemoncade are released here under GPLv3.

  Mines, Checkers, Tic-Tac-Toe, Blockfall, Connect Four, Dots and Boxes,
  Hangman, and Chess originated in or were adapted from OIAB. Network play,
  appliance profiles, server statistics, and network score submission were
  removed for these standalone browser editions.
  The public edition removes OIAB profiles, server statistics, and appliance APIs.
