Standalone Games - Source and License Notes

Minesweeper
  Adapted by the repository owner from the Overland In A Box (OIAB) project.
  OIAB has no public project license at the time of this adaptation.
  The public edition removes OIAB profiles, server statistics, and appliance APIs.

The public editions use no external runtime services or remotely loaded libraries.
Checkers, Tic-Tac-Toe, and Blockfall were adapted from the local OIAB project for standalone, browser-only play. Multiplayer, server profiles, and network score submission have been removed.
Connect Four, Dots and Boxes, and Hangman were similarly rebuilt as standalone local games from their OIAB counterparts.
Chess uses the chess.js rules engine bundled with OIAB and a local-only computer opponent. See the chess.js source header for its license terms.
Paddle Duel, Space Rocks, and Pinball were adapted from DaemonOS for standalone play. Pinball includes the Planck.js physics engine; its license notice remains in the bundled source.
Casey was adapted from DaemonOS with its original maze, character, input, rendering, and synthesized-audio systems.
Missiles Away, Chilopodophobia, Road Hopper, and Jungle Jumper were adapted from DaemonOS as local-only browser games.
