    How to play

    The land is your command.

    Essentials

    • Shape and settle: raise or lower terrain corners to make broad, dry plateaus. Autonomous walkers build the largest dwelling that local flat land supports.
    • Direct your people: Settle builds, Gather follows the beacon, Roam fights across the realm, and Fight + Settle attacks locally before building.
    • Keep watch: drag or use WASD to move, Z/X to rotate, and the overview to survey. H finds your largest group; B finds the largest active battle.
    • Win or lose: eliminate Veyra's people through ordinary conquest, or invoke Reckoning in Classic Contest. You lose when none of your people remain.
    Landscape and settlements
    • Raise / Lower: hover a joint for its coordinates and exact change. Click with a tool, or press R and F.
    • Fields: ordinary buildings occupy one plot. A Castle occupies 3×3 plots and needs a complete contiguous 5×5 field.
    • Obstacles: sink trees and dark stone to the waterline. Light stone must first be raised, then lowered. All block settlement sites and routes.
    • Damage and recovery: disturb or flood a supporting field and its dwelling downgrades or falls. Surplus residents emerge as walkers; restoring the field allows recovery.
    Orders, beacon, and champions
    • Gather: the leader follows the golden beacon and the tribe follows the leader.
    • Roam: people pursue reachable enemies across the world.
    • Fight + Settle: ordinary walkers pursue enemies within six tiles and eight route steps, then settle when clear. Champions patrol around the beacon instead of founding settlements.
    Powers and world rules
    • Previews: select a targeted power and hover to see its footprint, cost, and any reason it cannot fire. Click to commit; select again or press Escape to cancel.
    • Immediate powers: Deluge and Champion act immediately. Reckoning is unavailable in Mortal Contest.
    • World rules: select the world name for its full rule. Windscar Vale warns 12 simulated seconds before each tide; low ground then harms both peoples.
    Saving and returning
    • Active worlds autosave. Resume preserves the saved contest and world rules.
    • New game replaces the current slot only after confirmation. Storage and cross-tab conflicts warn and pause saving instead of silently overwriting progress.
