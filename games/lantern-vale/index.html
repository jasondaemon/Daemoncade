Lantern Vale
Mira's lantern is the last light in the vale. Cross three lands, descend into the wardens' halls, and return their three hearts to the northern beacon.
Alpha · 27 areas · local checkpoint saves
Traveler's guide
Move with arrows/WASD or hold the direction buttons. Face a shadow and strike with Space/J. L raises your lantern. Q drinks a tonic. E interacts with waystones, dungeon stairs and the final beacon. P pauses; R returns here without deleting your checkpoint.
Explore the western grove first. In each dungeon, carry light across the hidden bridge and step on the golden sun plate. Defeat the hall sentries for a key; defeat the warden and touch its heart. Wardens awaken in lantern light. Each heart strengthens your sword and opens the next land.
Save at glowing waystones before entering a dungeon and after returning. They restore health and sell one tonic for six coins. Saves remain on this browser and device; clearing site storage removes them. New journey does not replace a checkpoint until you save again.
