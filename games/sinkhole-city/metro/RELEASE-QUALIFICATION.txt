SINKHOLE CITY EXPEDITION -- 1.0.0-rc.2 / qualification52
Feature freeze and qualification pass, 2026-10-31

STATUS: LOCAL RELEASE CANDIDATE. NOT V1 STABLE. NOT PUBLICLY DEPLOYED.

Changes under the freeze
  * No worlds, modes, currencies or new rewards added. Saves, checkpoint
    schema, reward IDs, growth and capture rules are preserved.
  * Eleven rim previews no longer run on boot, ordinary world menus or
    results. Collection renders one missing preview per frame, cancels
    obsolete work, reuses its cache and degrades to icons on failure.
  * HUD text updates at 10 Hz instead of every rendered frame; simulation
    remains fixed at 60 Hz. Existing render-rate choices are preserved.
    Fresh primary-touch devices default to 30 FPS; 60 FPS stays selectable.
  * Alien terrain uses one blended surface instead of overlapping raised
    patches and a hard-edged basin. Source-material ownership survives
    ground batching, so world switches actually dispose the cloned material.
  * RC2 wrapper/version and package inventory match the current build.
    Music provenance records the owner's explicit redistribution clearance
    on 2026-10-31, alongside source and derivative hashes.

Pacing evidence (diagnostic, not human acceptance)
  All 18 contracts run across seeds 1, 7 and 23, using dimensions measured
  from loaded models and normal movement/capture. All 54 routes completed.
  Non-Infinity routes: approximately 42-154 seconds. Complete Infinity:
  approximately 826-894 seconds (14-15 minutes), with every object consumed.
  First intake: approximately 0.23-3.03 seconds. Longest observed gap between
  collections: 9.1 seconds. Detailed summary: qualification52-pacing.json.
  The driver knows every object's position and chooses eligible goals. These
  times do NOT prove new-player difficulty, enjoyment or recommended quotas.
  Quotas/growth/medal targets were deliberately not changed from this data.

Human acceptance still required before Stable
  1. iPhone browser AND Home Screen: at least 15 minutes, world switching,
     portrait/landscape, lock/unlock, background interruption, force-close,
     paused checkpoint restoration, music/effects mute and save export/import.
  2. One mid-range Android device and Safari/Firefox desktop smoke checks.
     No Android device was available to this local agent.
  3. Five uncoached new-player observations, plus a complete Infinity run.
     Record confusion, dull travel, voluntary replay, model recognition and
     whether the final empty ending feels earned. Human art/pacing approval
     remains open; automated route completion cannot supply it.
  4. On the actual deployed origin: install/update, preserved saves, backup
     restore, suspension, network failure/retry and verified rollback build.
  Soundtrack rights hold is CLOSED by the owner's 2026-10-31 confirmation.

Release procedure
  Do not overwrite the public beta or mark Stable from test counts. Keep
  the previous RC1 snapshot. Build RC2 into a fresh directory, independently
  serve it, run the qualification scripts, obtain the above sign-offs, then
  make a deliberate catalog/app cutover with the old build retained.
  Offline play is not promised. Portable exports contain campaign medals,
  Arena/exploration records, not an in-progress run. A new local port has a
  separate storage origin; it does not inherit the 4197 preview's progress.
