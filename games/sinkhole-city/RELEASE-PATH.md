# Sinkhole City: path to 1.0

Owner: Daemoncade · Established 2026-10-29 · Release: 1.0.0 Stable (owner-approved 2026-10-31)

## Product direction

Make swallowing a world feel physical, readable and rewarding on a phone. Preserve the five environment identities and existing beta while a new Metro district establishes the visual standard. The new presentation uses the repository's local Three.js runtime, a fixed elevated camera, consistent model footprints and a lightweight simulation on the ground plane. Consumption uses active contact dynamics and scene-specific collapse animation; a full rigid-body engine is optional, not a requirement for every prop.

## Critical direction: earn the next run (`payoff26`)

No feature checklist can establish that this will be a hit. The current build has functioning systems, but its appeal is still undermined by repetitive quotas, abrupt endings, restrained payoff, uneven world art and unqualified mobile performance. It needs a recognizable pleasure: **start small, find a good feeding route, and leave a miniature world visibly empty**.

The reference distinction matters: [Hole.io's official presentation](https://voodoo.io/games/hole-io) emphasizes easy movement, scale growth and satisfying world consumption; [Donut County](https://www.donutcounty.com/) emphasizes authored physics situations and character. These are product references, not evidence about their internal implementation or retention. Our opportunity is expressive, explorable worlds with physical intake and memorable chain reactions, without ads, currencies or notification pressure.

Priority and execution order:

1. **Payoff and a reason to finish clearing — this pass.** Keep an already-grown hole and its consumed scene after a contract win; offer an optional untimed continuation rather than forcing a restart or a victory timer. Show honest world coverage during play, a proper result breakdown, largest catch and best feeding chain. Retain exploration personal bests per world, including unfinished Free Roam sessions. Celebrate rare chain milestones through bounded local effects and short procedural audio, never instructional overlays. Preserve contract timings, medals and Arena scoring.
2. **Author memorable routes and events.** Audit the opening 30 seconds of each world with humans. Add geographically coherent alternate feeding routes and distinct, discoverable interactions: cascading city courtyards/deliveries, campsite setup/payoff, marine behavior and habitat interactions, alien relay choices, orbital collapse. Do not make every world the same crate-opening mechanic or obscure required targets. Prototype one encounter at a time and verify contacts, readability and replay value before multiplying content.
3. **Replay variety, once the loop is enjoyable.** Add a separate seeded score challenge with disclosed conditions and comparable records; keep story/medal progress independent. Validate route alternatives before adding random modifiers. Avoid a larger checklist of modes on the opening screen.
4. **Production art and sound.** Apply a consistent silhouette/material/detail standard to landmarks, props and rims; replace conspicuously placeholder models. Build a restrained world-specific soundscape with independent controls, then tune on phone speakers. Preserve small-screen readability and triangle/draw budgets.
5. **Release evidence.** New-player onboarding observation without recurring prompts; five-world full-clear and interrupted-save tests; real iPhone and Android 15-minute performance/thermal tests; Safari/Firefox, accessibility, asset packaging and install routing. Public release still requires explicit acceptance.

Acceptance for this first slice: a win can continue with exactly the same run/objects/size; its contract result cannot be awarded again during exploration; a 100% clear is distinct from a contract win; personal bests survive export/import; pause, background suspension and reduced/low effects remain respected; no compulsory waiting or power purchase is added. The next human review should ask whether players voluntarily keep clearing, whether the ending feels rewarding, and where they lose interest—not whether the new code runs.

Executed first slice: dedicated coverage/catch/chain results, optional same-scene untimed continuation, per-world exploration personal bests with pause checkpoints and validated backup migration, quiet live coverage, and bounded local chain effects/procedural reward audio. Actual-geometry full-clear routes pass in every world (approximately 136–191 simulated seconds); contract records remain immutable during continuation. This does not implement the encounter/route, seeded-challenge, art or device-qualification stages above.

Next playtest: observe five new players without coaching. Record first successful intake time, points of control/size confusion, voluntary continuation/retry choices and the first moment of boredom. Ask what memorable event they recall afterward. Inspect device frame-time/memory/thermal behavior rather than extrapolating desktop software-rendered triangle counts. These observations determine the next encounter/art iteration; do not claim a retention rate or a hit without that evidence.

## Audit baseline

The exposed game is a two-minute solo run. Five scenes, ambient animation, sound, a floating joystick, minimap and per-scene best scores exist. Opponent code is unfinished and inaccessible from the menu. There are no contracts, world progression or meaningful solo success thresholds.

Browser inspection and 25 simulated two-minute routes identified:

- All 83 images are requested at startup: approximately 28.4 MiB of resources excluding music, representing approximately 134 MiB of raw RGBA pixels. This is not a measurement of retained process memory.
- Movement clamps frame time to 34 ms while the round clock uses wall time. A controlled five-second movement check covered 1,321 units at 60 FPS but 434 at 10 FPS.
- Pausing preserves held input. The game's app-wrapper suspension message is unhandled. Hiding during countdown leaves music running and permits the round to start.
- Consumption spirals and scales sprites rather than showing depth. Perspective, visible size and edible radius are not consistently related.
- Five greedy routes per world reached radius 92 in 40–49 seconds in Alien World and 102–118 seconds in Orbit Collapse. These are diagnostic routes, not human difficulty estimates.
- Solo results always declare the local player a winner. Save writes are unguarded.

- Free Roam: untimed clearing and discovery.
- Medals unlock later worlds; mastery unlocks cosmetic rims, trails and celebrations. Avoid permanent power advantages in competitive records.
- Records separate world, mode, difficulty and rules version. Existing beta best scores remain available as legacy records.
- No online multiplayer or daily-service dependency for 1.0. Daily seeded challenges are a later candidate.

## Architecture and performance

- Separate simulation, world definitions, renderer, input, audio, persistence and interface. Use seeded layout generation and an explicit simulation clock.
- Use spatial cells for nearby edible-target queries. Batch static props and repeated geometry, pool effects, and keep allocation out of hot loops.
- For the 3D district, cut the ground at the hole with a shader and render an interior below it. Ground depth naturally occludes descending objects. Eligibility comes from an authored footprint; animation never silently changes the gameplay fit rule.
- Prefer repository models and verified CC0 packs with a local license manifest. Three.js instancing reduces repeated-geometry draw calls, but does not replace profiling.
- Load only the active environment, release obsolete image/model references, and load later worlds on demand. M2 initial playable transfer target: 6–8 MB, measured on a cold load.
- Target sustained 60 FPS on modern phones with an intentional stable 30 FPS option. Render resolution and effects may scale; movement, timing and eligibility may not.
- Use material/mass-based audio, separate music/effects preferences, bounded voices and complete background suspension.
