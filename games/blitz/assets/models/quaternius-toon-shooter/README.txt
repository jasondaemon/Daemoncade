The source models include their geometry, materials, skeletons, and animation
data. Their embedded buffers were externalized to local .bin files for reliable
browser loading. Blitz! uses Run_Shoot, Idle_Shoot, and Death animations.
Runtime preparation batches compatible material primitives with vertex colors,
shares duplicate skeletons within each character, and selects one weapon from
the included armory. Original source assets remain unchanged.
