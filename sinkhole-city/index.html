Sinkhole City

Start as a pothole. Swallow cones, coolers, cars, cabins, and anything else small enough to fit.

Touch-drag anywhere, or use WASD / arrow keys.

How to Play

Grow before the clock runs out.

Move your sinkhole around the world. Swallow smaller objects to grow. A bigger hole unlocks bigger targets, and chaining objects earns combo points.

Objects with a red bounce are still too large. Come back after eating smaller targets.
